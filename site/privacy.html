Privacy policy

Privacy

28 October 2026

Lazy Scan collects nothing. Your scans stay on your device, and there are no accounts, no analytics and no ads. This page covers the iPhone app.

What the app stores

Scans, photos, ID card pages and the PDFs and image files made from them are kept on your iPhone, in the app's own storage and in the folders you save to. The app also keeps its settings, the addresses and certificates of scanners you added, and the pairing with your desktop app. None of it leaves the device unless you send it somewhere yourself. Deleting the app deletes its own storage; files you saved to folders stay where you put them.

What the app sends

We run no servers, so there is nothing for the app to send us. It talks over your local network in two cases, and only when you use them:

  • to your own AirScan (eSCL) printer-scanner, to find it and fetch the pages you scan;
  • to your own computer, if you paired the iPhone with the Lazy Scan desktop app, to send it the pages.

Sharing a file through the share sheet, or saving it to a folder or an app such as Mail, is handled by iOS and the app you pick.

Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph pages, and to scan the pairing code shown by the desktop app. Pictures are not uploaded.
  • Local network: to find and use your scanner and your paired computer. The app does not look at other devices on the network.
  • Photo library (add only): when you choose Save Image in the share sheet, the image is written to Photos. Importing works the other way round through the system picker: the app receives only the photos you pick and never reads the rest of your library.

The page-finding model (DocAligner) and ONNX Runtime run on the device.

Children

There are no accounts and the app collects no data, so there is nothing about a child to collect or to delete.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts to do something that changes the above, this page is updated first and the date at the top changes.
